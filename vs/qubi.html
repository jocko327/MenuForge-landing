Where MenuForge is the better fit

You run an independent restaurant or brewery in the US, you want Toast to talk to the recipe, and you want an invoice in the inbox to recost the plate — including the garlic salt hiding in four nested batches. You will not wait on an EU-first HACCP suite to document the menu.

Where Qubi may fit better

You want inventory, waste KPIs, a digital allergen book, fridge-temperature HACCP, and supplier orders emailed from the same cheap euro seat. That is Qubi’s lane. It is not a Toast kitchen in Portage.
