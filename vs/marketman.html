MenuForge vs MarketMan

MarketMan is restaurant inventory software for cost control. MenuForge is recipe-first: know the plate cost before the ticket prints, then keep that number honest when GFS or Sysco changes a case price.

MenuForge MarketMan
Starting point Recipes and plate cost Inventory and COGS
Entry price $79.95/mo Essentials (Free for 5 recipes) About $199/mo on public listings
Setup Self-serve. No setup fees. Often a longer implementation
POS / accounting Toast POS live; others on the roadmap Leads with POS and accounting partners
Invoices Email or upload; recipes recost Inventory and purchasing workflow

Where MenuForge is the better fit

  • You are an independent restaurant or brewery. You need food cost down to the penny, not a full inventory stack.
  • You will not pay enterprise money or wait six months to document the menu.
  • You want AI to draft a special against the pantry you already stock, then see contribution margin before it hits the board.

Where MarketMan may fit better

If you need inventory-first purchasing, a wide POS and accounting logo wall, and you have the budget and time for that category, MarketMan is built for that job. MenuForge does not claim to replace a full inventory suite.

Less than a quarter of what MarketMan charges at the public entry price. No setup fees, no six-month implementation.
