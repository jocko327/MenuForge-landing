MenuForge vs MarginEdge

MarginEdge is an all-inclusive restaurant back office: invoices, bill pay, inventory, ordering, a daily controllable P&L, and recipe costing. They publish $350 / loc month-to-month, or about $315 if you prepay the year. Freepour, their liquor SmartScale, is another $150 plus a one-time scale setup. This page compares that suite to MenuForge AI+ at $139.95/mo.

MarginEdge prices from marginedge.com/pricing (checked September 2026): $350 per location monthly, 10% off annual, Freepour $150 extra plus scale setup. They do not charge a traditional “turn it on” fee; they sell onboarding packages instead. Toast locations also pay Toast’s Restaurant Management Suite API fee (~$50 / loc) as a pass-through. MenuForge AI+ is $139.95/mo with no setup invoice.

Where MenuForge is the better fit

You want the plate cost on the open recipe, an AI draft against your pantry, and invoice recosting without standing up a full back office. You do not need bill pay, a daily P&L, or a liquor scale. You will not wait on an implementation package to document the menu.

Where MarginEdge may fit better

You want the books and the walk-in in one product. MarginEdge is built for operators who process every invoice, pay vendors from the same screen, count inventory, compare theoretical vs actual usage, and read a daily controllable P&L fed by POS sales and labor. Multi-unit groups get commissary transfers and accounting sync (QuickBooks and similar). A bar program that needs Freepour — scan the bottle, weigh it, sync the pour — is their lane, not ours. That stack is $350 a location before the scale, and it does not write a special from a vibe.
