Where MenuForge is the better fit

You need to know how much garlic salt is in the burger after four nests, and you need the case price from Friday’s invoice — not a typed-in $1.89 that nobody updates. Toast match, dietary flags, and a team that can open the file without you.

Where KitchenPro may fit better

You have a binder of cards, you want them typed in from a photo for $24, and you do not need invoices, POS, or anyone else on the account. That is a cheaper recipe box. It is not the costing system.
