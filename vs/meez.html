Where MenuForge is the better fit

You want invoice-driven costing and an AI draft of the next special for less than meez Premium’s $199, and you run Toast or no POS at all.

Where meez may fit better

You want a built-in yield and unit database instead of building your own pantry, visual prep training, USDA nutrition labels, or a Restaurant365 sync. meez does those well, and MenuForge does not do them yet.
