MenuForge vs MarginLeaks

MarginLeaks is margin analytics for independents: true plate cost, then the exact price each dish needs on DoorDash, Uber Eats, Grubhub, and dine-in after the commission you actually pay. They publish one plan at $79/mo ($790/year). This page compares that seat to MenuForge AI+ at $139.95/mo.

MarginLeaks prices from their public schema and site (checked September 2026): $79 monthly or $790 annual — twelve months for the price of ten. They describe five inputs under the plate: recipes, ingredients, invoices, sales mix, and delivery settlements. Invoice capture is photograph-and-approve, not hands-off. They list Toast and Square POS, and unattended invoice reading, as coming in v2. MenuForge AI+ is $139.95/mo.

Where MenuForge is the better fit

You run the kitchen in the tool: write or generate the recipe, keep vendor packs and dietary flags honest, email in the invoice, and recost the board. You want AI to draft a special against the pantry and a caption from the card. Delivery math is not the product. The plate is.

Where MarginLeaks may fit better

You already know the recipes and you are leaking money on the apps. MarginLeaks is built for the dish that is $14 on the menu, $6.10 after food and a penciled 25% cut, then $4.60 after the invoice moves and the settlement shows you were on Premier at 30%. They back-calculate the real commission from payouts, then hand you the exact DoorDash / Uber Eats / Grubhub price that protects dine-in profit — with the volume-risk of a steep markup written down. The Monday Margin Health report ranks drifted dishes by dollars, not gut feel. That is a cheaper, narrower job than MenuForge. It does not generate the dish, match Toast, or post the special.
