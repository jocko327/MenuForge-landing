Changelog

Major customer-facing features and the month they shipped. Bug fixes stay out of this list. Dates are when the work landed in the product, not when we wrote the ad.

  1. September 2026

    • Top 10 spend and year-over-year case drift. Receipts ranks the cases you buy the most and shows how far each one moved, so you can send the list to the distributor.
    • Reviewed pack prices that stay put. Confirm a line on a receipt and MenuForge keeps that price instead of letting the next OCR pass guess again.

  2. August 2026

    • Toast POS. Menu sync, recipe match, and sales into the kitchen — including modifiers that share one recipe.
    • Receipt processing rebuild. Email or upload an invoice. Pack costs flow into the pantry and every recipe that uses them recosts.
    • Inventory counts and par sheets. Count by storage area. Pars use estimated on-hand instead of another weekend in a spreadsheet.

  3. June 2026

    • Post a dish to Facebook. AI copy from the recipe card, in your voice, with the photo already in the app.

  4. Already in the product

    • Live plate cost on the open recipe, plus minimum sell price and contribution margin.
    • Nested batch recipes. House seasoning inside the beef inside the patty inside the burger — garlic salt counted all the way up.
    • Dietary flags that roll up from ingredients, including batches.
    • AI recipe generation with Cost Target, Pantry Usage, and Insanity sliders, plus menu descriptions and cup-to-case math.
    • Vendor variants on one ingredient, so a GFS case and a Sysco bag do not fight over which cost is real.
