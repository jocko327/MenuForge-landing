Toast POS integration for recipe costing

Connect Toast and MenuForge pulls your menu and sales. Match Toast items and modifiers to recipes once, and see what each item sold against what it costs to make. Toast is included on the AI+ and Unlimited plans, and in the free trial. Each MenuForge account connects to one Toast location.

What syncs

  • Menus. Toast menu items come into MenuForge so you can match them to recipes. MenuForge suggests matches by name and you confirm them.
  • Modifiers. Link modifier choices (add bacon, sub a gluten-free bun) to the recipes or ingredients they add, so a modifier’s cost is counted.
  • Sales. Orders sync nightly. See units sold per item and which sales are not matched to a recipe yet.
  • Menu engineering. Put sales volume next to plate cost and margin to see which items carry the menu and which drag it.

What it does not do

  • MenuForge reads from Toast. It does not push menu or price changes back to Toast.
  • It does not replace xtraCHEF or Toast’s own back office. See MenuForge vs xtraCHEF.
  • Toast is the only live POS integration. Square, Clover, and others are on the roadmap. See integrations.

You do not need Toast to use MenuForge

Recipe costing, invoices, and AI work without any POS. Toast adds the sales side.

Questions

Which plans include the Toast integration?

AI+ and Unlimited, plus the 7-day free trial.

Does MenuForge work with Square or Clover?

Not yet. Toast is the only live POS integration. Other POS systems are on the roadmap.

How often do Toast sales sync?

Orders sync nightly, and you can run a sync on demand.
