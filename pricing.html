Frequently Asked Questions

Can I change plans later?

Yes! You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Upgrades take effect immediately, and downgrades will apply at the end of your current billing period.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover) through our secure payment processor, Stripe.

How does the annual billing discount work?

When you choose annual billing, you save 10% compared to paying monthly. You'll be billed once per year at the discounted rate.

What is the recipe limit?

Essentials and AI+ plans include up to 300 recipes (including archived). You can expand your storage with a recipe add-on pack: +500 recipes for +$20/mo or +1,000 recipes for +$50/mo. The Unlimited plan has no recipe limits.

How do AI recipe credits work?

The AI+ plan includes 25 AI recipe generation calls per month. Each request to generate a recipe counts as one call, regardless of how many recipes AI returns. Follow-up conversation after a recipe is returned does not count against your limit. If you need more, you can purchase additional credits: 5 for $20 or 10 for $35 as a one-time charge.

What AI features are included?

The AI+ and Unlimited plans include AI recipe generation, unlimited AI-powered recipe description generation, unlimited AI unit conversion assistance, receipt processing (email or upload invoices with live ingredient prices and price-change alerts), and posting recipes to Facebook with AI copy written in your brand voice. The AI+ plan includes 25 AI recipe calls per month, while the Unlimited plan has no limits. Receipt processing and Facebook posting are not included on Free or Essentials.

Can I cancel my subscription anytime?

Absolutely. You can cancel your subscription at any time from your account settings. You'll retain access until the end of your current billing period, then automatically downgrade to the Free plan.

What's the difference between Essentials and AI+?

Both plans include 300 recipes and core recipe management features. AI+ adds AI-powered recipe generation (25/month), unlimited AI descriptions, unlimited unit conversions, custom branding, receipt processing, and posting recipes to Facebook. AI+ also supports up to 6 users vs. 3 on Essentials. Free and Essentials do not include receipt processing or Facebook posting.
