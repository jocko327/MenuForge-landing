Best recipe costing software for independent restaurants

If you searched “recipe costing software,” you already know the spreadsheet goes stale the week a case price moves. Independent restaurants and breweries need the plate cost on the open recipe, and they need it to move when the invoice comes in.

What to look for

  • Live plate cost, minimum sell price, and contribution margin while the recipe is open.
  • Sub-recipes that roll up. A demi or house vinaigrette should not quietly wreck food cost.
  • Invoices that update pack costs. Print-and-sticky-note is how you go underwater on wings after the weekend.
  • Recipes that stay when a cook walks. If they live only in someone’s head, they can walk out the door.
  • A price you can pay without a six-month implementation.
