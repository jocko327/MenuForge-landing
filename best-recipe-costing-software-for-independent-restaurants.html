Why the AI column is the gap

The other tools on this table will cost a recipe you already wrote. MenuForge AI+ writes the draft, costs it against your pantry, writes the menu description, does the cup-to-case math, and posts the dish. That is the work that used to sit in a spreadsheet, a binder, and a Facebook tab.

Where someone else still wins

Need kitchen training videos and a culinary system of record? Look at meez. Selling hard on DoorDash, Uber Eats, and Grubhub, and you want the exact reprice per platform from your real settlements? MarginLeaks is cheaper and narrower — that is their job. Want AP and inventory that live inside Toast’s own website? xtraCHEF Pro — MenuForge still talks to Toast over the API. Need a daily controllable P&L, unlimited bill pay, inventory counts, theoretical vs actual, or a liquor SmartScale? MarginEdge is the $350 back-office suite. MenuForge does not pretend to be those products. It is the only one that checks the AI rows.
