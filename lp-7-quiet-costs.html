7 quiet costs of scattered recipes and guesswork

The 7-cost checklist. If recipes still live in a binder, a Drive folder, and one cook’s head, the margin is already leaking — even when the line looks busy.

Put recipes and costing in one place Free forever for up to 5 costed recipes. No credit card.
  1. 1

    Recipes in too many places

    Binder, shared Drive, a cook’s Notes app. Cheese jumps. Half the versions never hear about it, so the plate you think you sell is not the plate you make.

  2. 2

    Plate cost you guessed

    Costing from memory or last week’s invoice while the POS still rings last year’s price. Food cost % looks fine until you cost one real dish.

  3. 3

    Prep that never rolls up

    House sauces, doughs, and batches sit at $0 because they are not linked as ingredients. Every plate that uses them is understated.

  4. 4

    Units that do not convert

    Ounces on the recipe, cases on the invoice, a blank unit on the line. Demand drops off the par sheet without anyone noticing.

  5. 5

    POS sales that never hit a recipe

    Unmatched or ignored tickets. Theoretical food cost stays pretty. Inventory and prep become fiction.

  6. 6

    Price drift three invoices later

    The same item, up 14%. Nobody changed the menu. Quiet cost creep hides until the month is already gone.

  7. 7

    The walk-out

    The only current recipe lives in one person’s head. They leave on a Friday. The margin — and the consistency — leave with them.
