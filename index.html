Description

A bright, aromatic pasta tossed with pan-seared chicken, fresh lemon zest, and garden herbs. Light enough for summer, satisfying enough for any season.

IngredientAmountUOMCost

1 Season chicken with salt, pepper, and lemon zest. Pan-sear in olive oil over medium-high heat, 6 min per side until golden and 165 F internal.
2 Cook pasta in salted boiling water until al dente (8-10 min). Reserve 1/2 cup pasta water, then drain.
3 Rest chicken 5 min, then slice. Toss pasta with sliced chicken, lemon juice, minced garlic, fresh basil, and reserved pasta water.
4 Top with shaved parmesan and a drizzle of olive oil. Serve immediately.
