Questions operators ask first

What is MenuForge?

MenuForge is web-based recipe costing software for independent restaurants and breweries. It tracks ingredient pack prices, reads vendor invoices with AI, and recosts every recipe and sub-recipe automatically. Plans start at $79.95/month, with a 7-day free trial.

How much does recipe costing software cost?

MenuForge Essentials is $79.95/month, AI+ is $139.95/month, and Unlimited is $189.95/month. Annual billing saves 10%. Every plan starts with a 7-day free trial of AI+, no credit card required.

Can I import my recipe spreadsheet?

Not yet. MenuForge does not import Excel or CSV recipe workbooks today. Most kitchens start with their top 10 dishes: add the ingredients once, and AI can draft a recipe from a short description so you are not retyping every tab.

Does MenuForge work with QuickBooks or Restaurant365?

Not yet. Toast POS is the only live integration. You can export recipes and costs to CSV for your accountant, and other POS and accounting tools are on the roadmap.

Which plan includes invoice and receipt processing?

Receipt processing is included on AI+ and Unlimited, and in the free trial. Essentials does not include it.

Does the AI calculate my food cost?

No. AI drafts recipes, descriptions, and unit conversions. Plate cost always comes from your own pack prices and invoices, and you review an AI draft before it is saved.

Can I use MenuForge for more than one location?

Each MenuForge account covers one location and one Toast connection. Restaurant groups with several locations should book a walkthrough so we can talk through setup.
