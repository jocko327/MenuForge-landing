Recipes

Your whole menu, at a glance

Filter by category, search, and sort, with dietary flags right on the card. The colors do the triage for you: a pink card means the costing is broken, usually a missing price or a unit that will not convert. A red border means the prep instructions are missing, and gold means AI wrote it. You spot every problem without opening a single recipe.

Pantry

One pantry, every vendor

Pack sizes, units, and vendor variants for each ingredient live in one place. Filter by supplier to see exactly what you buy and what it costs, then keep those pack prices flowing into every recipe that uses them. When a vendor swaps a case size, you update it once and the rest of the menu stays honest.

Costing

Live plate cost on every line

Every ingredient costs itself out as you type. Cost, minimum sell price, and your menu price stay pinned to the header, so the plate cost is never a guess when you print the ticket. Nested batch recipes roll up too, so a sauce used in ten dishes still carries the right cost into each one.

Receipts

Invoices in, costs updated

Email or upload a vendor invoice. Confirm the unmatched lines and live pack costs flow straight into your pantry, then every recipe that uses those ingredients recosts itself without a spreadsheet pass.

Price history

See costs move before they hurt

Track pack cost over time per ingredient, with the percent change called out so a quiet creep does not sneak past. When a price updates, every recipe using it recosts automatically. Spot the spike early, then decide whether to raise the menu price, swap the vendor, or rewrite the build.
