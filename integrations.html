Does this connect to what I already use?

MenuForge focuses on recipe costing and ingredient tracking. Toast POS is live. Other POS and accounting integrations are on our roadmap. Export recipes and costs to CSV for your accountant.

Toast POS — live

Connect Toast from the app. MenuForge syncs Toast menus, matches items to recipes, and pulls sales so you can see what actually sold against what it costs to make.

  • Menu sync from Toast
  • Match POS items to recipes (and unmatch if you got it wrong)
  • Order / sales pull for the dishes you have linked

Accounting and other POS — on the roadmap

We do not connect to QuickBooks, Restaurant365, MarketMan, or other POS systems yet. Export recipes and costs to CSV for your accountant. If you need a specific POS, join the waitlist and tell us which one.
