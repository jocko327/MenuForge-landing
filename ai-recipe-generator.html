AI recipe generator, costed against your own pantry

Describe a dish and MenuForge AI drafts the recipe, adds any missing ingredients and batch sub-recipes, and then MenuForge costs the plate against the pack prices you actually pay. You review every draft before it saves. AI recipe generation is on the AI+ plan (25 per month) and Unlimited, and the free trial includes 5.

Tune it before it writes a line

Three sliders tell the AI how to build the dish:

  • Cost target. How hard to push plate cost down.
  • Pantry usage. How much to lean on what you already stock instead of special orders.
  • Insanity. Classic and safe, or one unexpected twist.

What the AI does, and what it does not

  • It drafts recipes and nested batches from a short description.
  • It follows category instructions you save once (for example, how your kitchen builds a burger or a catering tray).
  • It writes menu descriptions and social captions from the finished recipe.
  • It never sets the cost. Plate cost always comes from your pantry pack prices and invoices.
  • Nothing saves until you review it.

Good uses

  • Drafting a weekly special that hits your food cost target with what is already on the shelf
  • Getting your existing dishes into MenuForge faster: describe the dish, then correct the quantities
  • Catering menus written for a set number of guests

Questions

Does the AI calculate food cost?

No. AI drafts the recipe. MenuForge calculates the plate cost from your own pack prices and invoices.

How many AI recipes do I get?

AI+ includes 25 AI recipe generations per month and Unlimited has no cap. The free trial includes 5. Extra credits can be purchased.

Can it use only ingredients I already stock?

Turn the Pantry usage slider up and the AI leans on ingredients already in your pantry.
