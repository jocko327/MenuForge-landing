Restaurant invoice processing that recosts every recipe

Email or upload a vendor invoice. AI reads every line, matches it to the right pantry ingredient, and updates the pack cost. Every recipe and sub-recipe that uses that ingredient recosts on its own. Receipt processing is on the AI+ ($139.95/mo) and Unlimited plans, and included in the free trial. Essentials does not include it.

Real result: Presidential Brewing used MenuForge invoice history to renegotiate their top 10 items and projects about $8,800 a year back. Read the case study.

How it works

  1. Send the invoice. Forward the distributor email or upload a photo or PDF.
  2. AI reads the lines. Item, pack size, quantity, and price are pulled from each line and matched to a pantry ingredient. Vendor item numbers help it match the same product next time.
  3. You confirm the leftovers. Anything AI could not place waits for you. Match it once and MenuForge remembers.
  4. Costs flow through. The new pack price lands on the ingredient with a dated price history, and every recipe that uses it recosts.

What you get out of the invoice history

  • Price-change alerts. See the items whose pack price jumped since the last invoice.
  • Price trends. Chart pack cost over time and spot creep in oils, proteins, and produce before the P&L does.
  • Top spend. Rank the items you spend the most on. That list is what Presidential Brewing took to their distributor.

What it does not do

MenuForge does not pay bills, sync invoices into QuickBooks or Restaurant365, or manage AP. It uses invoices to keep your recipe costs right. If you need bill pay and accounting sync, see MenuForge vs MarginEdge.

Questions

Which plan includes invoice processing?

AI+ and Unlimited. The 7-day free trial includes 10 receipts so you can test it on real invoices. Essentials does not include receipt processing.

Does it work with Sysco, US Foods, and GFS invoices?

Yes. AI reads the line items from the invoice image or PDF, so it works with any distributor invoice that lists items, pack sizes, and prices.

Does MenuForge sync invoices to QuickBooks?

No. Invoices are used to update recipe costs. Accounting integrations are on the roadmap.
